Privacy Policy

Updated: November 1, 2026

In short

Skrynia does not collect your data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and the developer cannot see it.

What is stored on your phone

Transactions, accounts, categories, debts, reminders, rules and Wallet payment data are kept in the app's local database, along with the backups the app makes. The developer has no servers, so there is nowhere for this data to go.

If iCloud or computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, iOS includes app data in that backup. This is governed by your iPhone settings and Apple's terms.

Network: exchange rates only

The app updates exchange rates by itself once a day, and also goes online when you update rates manually or import data. Requests contain no amounts, accounts or transactions.

Service Purpose What it receives
National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua) Exchange rates and their history Currency code, dates, IP address
CoinGecko Current crypto rates, and currency rates when the National Bank does not respond Coin names, currency codes, IP address
Binance Crypto rate history Coin pair, start date, IP address

Your IP address is covered by these services' own privacy policies. Links to this page and to the ready-made Shortcuts open in system apps.

iPhone features

Face ID: the app receives only a yes or no answer from iOS, never biometric data. Notifications are created on the phone itself. Wallet payments are passed on by a Shortcut you set up yourself: the amount, merchant and card stay on your phone. Siri requests are processed by Apple under its own terms.

Security

The app does not encrypt data separately: it is protected by your iPhone passcode and by the Face ID lock if you turn it on. Rate requests use HTTPS only.

What you send yourself

Export and Share send a file wherever you choose.

Feedback opens an email in your own mail app. Besides your text, it contains the app version, iOS version, iPhone model and language, and you see all of this before sending. The developer uses your address and message only to reply, and deletes them at your request.

How to delete your data

Settings → Backups → Clear data removes transactions, accounts and rates. Backups on the phone are deleted on the Saved backups screen.

Deleting the app removes everything from the phone. Files you exported yourself and iPhone backups stay wherever you keep them.

Your rights

The developer does not hold your financial data, so you can view, correct and delete it right in the app. If you emailed the developer, you can ask to see or delete that message. If you live in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your supervisory authority.

Children

Skrynia is not made specifically for children and collects no data about anyone, children included.
